The problem

A group's money only works if everyone trusts the treasurer

Who we build for: small groups in Poland that already pool money. Workplace kasy zapomogowo-pożyczkowe (KZP) in schools, offices and factories, teams with a "kasa koleżeńska", friends and families lending to each other.

Members save every month and borrow at 0%, with co-members vouching for the loan. It works only through intermediaries:

  • the treasurer holds the money and the books,
  • the board decides who gets a loan,
  • the employer's payroll deducts installments and is the only real enforcement.

When that trust fails

A PKZP treasurer in Jawor county took 61,000 zł of colleagues' savings and lost it on a crypto exchange.

Puls Legnicy, March 2024

A PKZP cashier hid 261,510 zł of withdrawals with falsified cash reports for ten years.

Court of Appeal in Katowice, II AKa 98/15

Informal groups have it worse: "the kasa" is simply someone's private bank account.

How it works

Save, borrow with guarantors, repay or be collected

1 · SAVE
Deposit into the vault

Anyone joins, no approval. Free savings are withdrawable any time.

2 · BORROW
Up to 2× savings

Own free savings lock first. The rest must be pledged by co-members.

3 · PAY OUT
100% covered → paid

The borrower pays the loan out to their wallet. Nobody signs off.

4 · REPAY
Installments at 0%

Each repayment unlocks the same amount of collateral, guarantors first.

Bartek borrows 1000 zł in 4 installments

His own savings 500 zł · Anna pledges 300 zł · Celina pledges 200 zł → collateral 1000 zł = loan, so he pays it out.

He repays once, then stops

The repayment frees 250 zł of the guarantors' pledges (3:2). After installment 2 + grace, Celina presses "Egzekwuj": 250 zł comes from Bartek's locked savings. Later installments take his last 250 zł, then 250 zł from Anna and Celina pro rata.

The fund ends whole

Bartek: kept the cash, lost 500 zł of savings.
Anna −150 zł, Celina −100 zł: the risk they chose.
Fund: 0 zł lost. Everyone withdraws the rest; vault ends at 0 zł. All on devnet (docs/devnet-e2e.md).

The moment the intermediary is no longer needed

An overdue installment is collected by anyone, with nobody's consent

// instructions/repay.rs: no signer required
pub fn handle_collect_overdue(ctx) {
  let overdue = loan.overdue(now,
      kasa.period_secs, kasa.grace_secs)?;
  require!(overdue > 0, NothingOverdue);
  check_guarantors(&loan, &guarantors)?;
  // borrower's savings first,
  // then guarantors pro rata
  seize(&mut loan, &mut borrower,
        &mut guarantors, overdue)?;
}

The tokens are already in the vault, so collection is bookkeeping on locked savings: it needs no signature from the borrower, the guarantors or us. It replaces the payroll deduction and the board's debt collection.

Why the fund can never lose

Two invariants, tested after every step

1. Every loan is fully covered

Pay-out requires locked savings = loan amount. Repayments release collateral; collection consumes it. Collateral always equals what is still owed.

2. The vault holds every free saving

vault = Σ savings − Σ lent

So anyone can withdraw what is theirs at any moment. A bank run cannot fail: the tests end with everyone withdrawing everything and the vault at 0.

The only risk is a chosen one

Only a guarantor who vouched for someone who didn't pay can lose, and at most the amount they pledged. That is the risk a KZP poręczyciel takes today, now explicit and visible to everyone.

Design reasoning

Why blockchain, and why no arbiter

Why not a database?

In a database the intermediary is whoever runs it: they can edit a balance, hold back a withdrawal or quietly "borrow" from the pot, which is how the cases on slide 2 happened. Here the balance is a program-owned account, the rules are code nobody can swap, and collection needs no trusted server. Solana makes it cheap enough for a 100 zł monthly contribution.

Why not an escrow marketplace?

We started there and dropped it. For physical goods someone must judge delivery: an arbiter (still an intermediary) or bonds that punish honest parties too. So we chose a relationship where the program sees every condition it enforces: deposits, balances, the schedule, the clock. Lending, the pattern Superteam calls the best example, in a form Polish workplaces already use.

Honest limits · next week

What it doesn't do yet, and what we'd build next

Limitations

  • Test złoty from a devnet faucet; the program takes any SPL mint (production: a stablecoin)
  • Members need a Solana wallet
  • Collection needs someone (or a bot) to send the transaction
  • Contributions are voluntary, savings earn no interest (as in a KZP)
  • The 2021 Act still expects a board; legal recognition needed
  • Not audited, devnet only

With another week

  • Standing orders without a bank: SPL delegation pulls installments and contributions
  • Zapomogi: grants released by member vote
  • PLN stablecoin, BLIK on-ramp, passkey wallets
  • Public collector bot and due-date reminders
  • Verifiable build, audit, mainnet
