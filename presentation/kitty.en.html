Kitty

The group kitty, minus the treasurer.

Savings, 0% loans and direct debits for groups – enforced by a Solana program, not by people.

The problem

Pooling money works only through someone you have to trust.

Coworkers, schools, friends and families already save together and lend to each other at 0%. It runs on a treasurer who holds the money, a board that approves loans and a payroll that deducts installments.

Kitty04
How it works

Save together. Borrow with guarantors. Let installments pay themselves.

01

Save

Members pay into the program's vault. A standing contribution can be pulled every period.

02

Borrow

Up to a multiple of your savings. Your free savings lock first; co-members pledge the rest.

03

Get paid

Once locked savings cover 100% of the loan, it pays out. Nobody approves it.

04

Repay

On the due date the installment leaves the wallet under a revocable SPL allowance.

Kitty05
No payroll, no bank

The installment leaves the wallet by itself.

The borrower approves a capped allowance once. On the due date a bot with no special rights sends the pull; the program takes only what is due. Revoke it, and the locked savings pay instead.

Kitty06
Why the fund never loses

vault = Σ savings − Σ lent

Every loan is fully covered before it pays out. A missed installment is taken from locked savings – the borrower's first, then the guarantors'. So every member's free money is always in the vault, ready to withdraw.
